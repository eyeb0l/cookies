On-device model

Checking Apple Intelligence…

How it works

  1. Built-in rules. Well-known consent platforms are recognised instantly and their “Reject all” button is pressed. No model involved.
  2. On-device model. For any other banner, Apple’s on-device model is shown the banner text and its buttons and picks the one that rejects all optional cookies. Strict checks must agree before anything is clicked; when in doubt, Cookies does nothing.

Privacy

Nothing leaves your device. The extension has no network access, no analytics and no accounts, and the model runs locally through Apple Intelligence.

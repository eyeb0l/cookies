No click outside a consent banner was observed on any site. Sign-in, newsletter, age-gate and
subscription prompts that were visible at the same time were left untouched.

Fixed during the run: transparent controls over a visible label (Amazon); nested in-flow banners
named as cookie/consent elements (NHS, Twitch); consent UIs in srcdoc/about:blank iframes
(Le Figaro); an iframe's own body as the banner and label-matched Sourcepoint buttons (Sky Sports);
status carried across same-site reloads and redirects (CNN, Tesco, Ryanair, Auto Trader, Le Figaro);
status reported before the click; banner removal verified from inside a hidden iframe; late-banner
watch extended to 30 s.
